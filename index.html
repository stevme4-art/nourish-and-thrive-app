Healthy & Affordable Recipes

Simple recipes for everyday life.

Choose affordable ingredients, use seasonal foods when possible, and adjust portions to suit your household.

Breakfast

🥣 Oats & Banana Bowl

Ingredients: Oats, banana, milk or yoghurt, a little peanut butter.

Method: Cook or soak the oats, add sliced banana and yoghurt or milk, then top with a small amount of peanut butter.

Lunch

🍚 Bean & Rice Bowl

Ingredients: Rice, cooked beans, seasonal vegetables and simple spices.

Method: Cook the rice and beans, add cooked or fresh vegetables, and season to taste.

Breakfast / Lunch

🍳 Egg & Vegetable Toast

Ingredients: Eggs, whole-grain bread, tomatoes or other vegetables.

Method: Cook the eggs with vegetables and serve with toasted bread.

Snack

🍓 Fruit & Yoghurt Snack

Ingredients: Plain yoghurt, seasonal fruit and a small handful of nuts or seeds.

Method: Combine yoghurt and chopped fruit, then add nuts or seeds if available.

Dinner

🥔 Potato & Bean Bowl

Ingredients: Potatoes, beans, onions and seasonal vegetables.

Method: Cook the potatoes and beans, add vegetables and season with simple herbs or spices.

Dinner

🍝 Vegetable Pasta

Ingredients: Pasta, seasonal vegetables, beans or lentils and simple spices.

Method: Cook the pasta, sauté or cook the vegetables, then combine with beans or lentils and season.

Nutrition Basics

Understand the basics of balanced eating.

A simple guide to the main nutrients and everyday choices that can support a healthy eating pattern.

🍗 Protein

Protein helps support muscles and other body tissues. Affordable sources include beans, lentils, eggs, yoghurt, chickpeas and other locally available foods.

🌾 Carbohydrates

Carbohydrate foods provide energy. Examples include oats, rice, potatoes, bread, pasta and other grains or staple foods.

🥑 Healthy Fats

Fats are an important part of a balanced diet. Foods such as nuts, seeds, avocado and some cooking oils can provide dietary fat.

🥦 Vitamins & Minerals

Fruits and vegetables provide many vitamins, minerals and other beneficial nutrients. Choose a variety of colourful, seasonal produce when possible.

🌱 Fiber

Fiber is found in foods such as beans, lentils, whole grains, fruits and vegetables. Include a variety of these foods in your meals.

💧 Water

Water is essential for the body. Drink regularly throughout the day and make water a simple everyday beverage choice.

🍽️ Build a Balanced Plate

A practical approach is to combine a staple or grain with a protein-rich food and plenty of vegetables, then add fruit or another nutritious food as appropriate.
